Commercial Framework

Engagement Terms & Conditions

Commercial framework, project engagement structure, payment terms, operational policies, liabilities, and professional service conditions for freelance and consultancy engagements.

1. Scope of Services

Services may include technical site leadership, integrated systems support, AV infrastructure coordination, construction interface management, commissioning support, live technical operations, consultation, troubleshooting, and project-based delivery services.

2. Commercial Terms

  • • Standard payment terms are strictly as agreed in writing prior to engagement.
  • • Late payments may result in suspension of services.
  • • Any additional scope outside agreed deliverables may be quoted separately.
  • • Expenses, accommodation, transport, permits, and specialist equipment are billable unless otherwise agreed.

3. Operational Responsibility

The client remains responsible for overall site safety, legal compliance, insurance, permits, and operational authorisations unless specifically contracted otherwise.

4. Liability

All services are provided in good faith and with professional care. Liability is limited to the value of the agreed engagement unless otherwise specified within a formal written contract.

5. Confidentiality

Project information, technical documentation, operational procedures, and client communications are treated as confidential unless disclosure is authorised in writing.

6. Cancellation & Scheduling

Confirmed bookings cancelled within short notice periods may be subject to cancellation fees depending on project allocation, scheduling impact, and committed resources.
