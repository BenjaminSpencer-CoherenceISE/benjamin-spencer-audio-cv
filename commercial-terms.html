Unless otherwise stated, travel, accommodation, visas, ground transport, freight, and per diem costs are excluded from standard rates and reimbursed separately.

09 / Invoicing & Payment

Standard payment terms are 30 days from invoice date unless otherwise agreed. Invoices may be issued weekly, per milestone, or upon project completion.

10 / Client Responsibilities

Clients shall ensure safe working conditions, timely provision of information, and availability of an authorised project contact for technical and commercial decisions.
