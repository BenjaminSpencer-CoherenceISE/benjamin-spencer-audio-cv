Landmark Project

Cartier — Louvre Abu Dhabi

Delivered AV, lighting, multimedia and interactive systems for the high-profile Cartier exhibition, Islamic Inspiration and Modern Design, at Louvre Abu Dhabi. Working within a highly secure museum environment with complex logistics and strict operational requirements, Benjamin led integrator coordination, troubleshooting, commissioning and systems support throughout delivery. Close collaboration with the main contractor and project stakeholders ensured seamless implementation of client directives and a world-class visitor experience within one of the region's most prestigious cultural venues.
